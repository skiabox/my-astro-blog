Hello, I'm Stavros Kefaleas, a full-stack software architect based in Larisa, Greece.

I have extensive experience designing and delivering web applications across the front end and back end. My recent work centers on React, TypeScript, Node.js, Express, MongoDB, Mongoose, PostgreSQL, and modern CSS systems such as Tailwind CSS.

- Node.js and Express services

- Technical leadership and team collaboration
